Fees and rules

How ordering on Forkline works. All prices are in pounds and include VAT.

Finding food

  • Search finds restaurants by their name, their cuisine or any dish on their menu.
  • "Deliver on" shows which restaurants are taking deliveries at the day and time you pick, using their opening hours, the days they're closed and any one-off closures.

Dishes and your cart

  • Sizes and extras are priced per item: two large pizzas pay the large supplement twice.
  • Choices marked "required" must be picked, and you can't pick more extras than a dish allows (for example "up to 3").
  • Offers shown on a dish are applied in your cart: with "2 for 1", every second portion is free.
  • You can order 1 to 20 of each dish. Sold-out dishes can't be ordered.
  • A cart holds dishes from one restaurant. Adding a dish from another restaurant asks you to start a new cart.
  • Your cart always uses the restaurant's current prices.

Fees

  • Delivery fee by distance: up to 2 km £1.49, up to 5 km £2.99, up to 8 km £4.49. We don't deliver further than 8 km.
  • Service fee: 5% of your food, at least £0.50 and at most £2.50.
  • Small-order fee: £1.50 when your food comes to less than £12.
  • Each restaurant has a minimum order. It counts your food only (before discounts), not the fees.
  • The total in your cart is what you'll pay before any tip, points or extras chosen at checkout: food, discounts, delivery, service and small-order fees.
  • Tips are optional and go to your courier in full. A percentage tip is worked out on your food alone.

Promo codes

  • One promo code per order. Codes come off your food, and "up to" amounts are the most a code can take off.
  • A code works until the end of its last day. Minimum spends count your food.
  • "First order" codes only work if you've never had a Forkline order before.

Points

  • Earn 1 point for every whole £1 of food (after discounts) when an order is delivered. Tips and fees don't earn points.
  • At checkout, every 100 points take £1 off your food. Points you use come off your balance straight away.

Delivery slots and addresses

  • Forkline is booked ahead: choose a half-hour slot from tomorrow up to 7 days ahead.
  • Slots run from the restaurant's opening time until 30 minutes before it closes. Late-night restaurants keep delivering after midnight until 30 minutes before they close.
  • Restaurants don't deliver on days they're closed, including one-off closures shown on their page.
  • We deliver to postcodes FL1 to FL6.

Your orders

  • You can only see your own orders.
  • You can cancel an order until the restaurant accepts it.
  • Reorder puts the same dishes, with the same sizes and extras, back in your cart.
  • Receipts show every fee, discount and tip, and the total you paid. Prices include VAT at 20%, so the VAT on a receipt is one sixth of your food after discounts.

Reviews and missing items

  • You can review an order once it's been delivered, once per order.
  • Something missing? Tell us within 48 hours of delivery. We refund what you paid for each missing item, up to the number you ordered.

For restaurants

  • Restaurant accounts see and manage only their own restaurants' orders.
  • Menu prices go from £0.50 to £100.00. A dish marked sold out can't be ordered until you mark it available again.
  • The daily sales report lists that day's orders that weren't rejected or cancelled, with their food total.
